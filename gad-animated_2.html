The Story

A fava-bean cart that became a national table.

GAD opened at Raml Station in Alexandria, selling ful medames and ta'meya to commuters headed for the tram. The bread, the tahini, and the way the beans are seasoned haven't changed — what's grown is everything around them: koshari counters, charcoal grills, molokhia, and a fiteer oven in nearly every branch.
